# Thinking of making a game about bees

![Concept art](/images/bee-game-concept-art.png)

[Forestry](https://modrinth.com/mod/forestry-community-edition) is an old mod for Minecraft that involves
a bee breeding system from which you can harvest resources, starting with honey and if you go far enough
you can get bees that produce mob drops, or even resources like iron or diamonds.

It's not my mod, but I've wanted to run with this idea as a full game for a while now, which is odd
given that the bees of that mod are notoriously grindy. It may have changed in the community edition,
I haven't played it recently my memory is from 2016 ish.

And I've had another idea which involves limited resources and dimension hopping. Initialy I wanted
to have a website where every /dimension/id url is a valid place to go to and collect resources,
but beyond the gimmick I never had any decent gameplay to go with it.

So I'm thinking each dimension holds some resources, and you can go to a dimension to collect some or all
of them, and bring them back to your own (seperate from the resource pool of dimensions). This creates a
technically limited resource pool, which I find interesting. And you can use these resources to build up
your own island.

Also, instead of having the player load a new url, the dimensions could be spread out on a map to look like 
a galaxy, which ties into my fascination with space.

I want it to be like those facebook mobile games that are full of microtransactions, without becoming unbearable.
I'm thinking, energy system, doing anything takes energy, your energy recovers every day but you can pay for more.
Although, I hate those energy systems. Just let me play the damn game. So how I monitize it is still up for debate.
It will be a live service game served on the web with an installable [PWA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_web_app),
but I want to at least try and avoid being predatory with monitization until it's bought from me for a billion dollars.

Regardless, I want some of the proceeds to go to charities related to insect conservation, which will ease my soul.

I need to figure out the gameplay loop still I think, going for rare bees and letting players show off their collection
will be fun I think, but I need to give them something to do with the resources the bees produce (crafting maybe?)
and something to do in-bewteen waiting for a rare drop that will be fun. I don't want to make minecraft, so probably not 
building. Then again, people play all those shitty mobile farming games, so maybe it's not that hard and it's just a case
of marketing.
